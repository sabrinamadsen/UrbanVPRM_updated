# Updated Urban Vegetation Photosynthesis and Respiration Model (UrbanVPRM)
# 500m branch

## Descriptions:
Scripts for the updated UrbanVPRM biospheric model. Used to model NEE over urban areas. Hourly NEE fluxes are available at 500 m x 500 m grid spacing. 

Methodology is based on [*Hardimann et al*., 2017], [*Gourdji et al*., 2021], [*Winbourne et al*., 2021] and [*Madsen-Colford et al*.,submitted] . Please contact Sabrina Madsen-Colford (smadsen@physics.utoronto.ca) if you have any comments. Thank you.

## Details:
Please refer to UrbanVPRM_instructions.pdf and [*Madsen-Colford et al*.,submitted] for details and required input datasets. 

## Reference:
Madsen-Colford, S., Hutyra, L., Smith, I., Wu, D., Arain, M. A., Staebler, R., Ma, W., Restrepo-Coupe, N., and Wunch, D.: Modification and comparison of two urban vegetation models over Southern Ontario, Canada, submitted to _JGR Biogeosciences_.
